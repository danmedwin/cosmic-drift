Cosmic Drift Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Cosmic Drift and the Cosmic Workshop are made by Dan Medwin. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Data collection

Cosmic Drift does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts, and no sign-in. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other device sensor. It makes no network requests while you play.

What is stored on your device

Your progress and creations are saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. This includes:

  • Saved games, high scores, and highest level reached
  • Custom levels, block designs, visual effects, UFO and ship designs, and plasma designs made in the Workshop
  • Settings such as sound preferences and whether you have seen the tutorial

None of this leaves your device. It is not backed up or synced by the app, and the developer cannot see it.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything the app has stored at any time in either of two ways:

  • Uninstall the app. Deleting Cosmic Drift from your device removes all of its stored data.
  • Erase All Data. Builds that include the "For Testers" menu offer an "Erase All Data" button that wipes all levels, designs, scores, and settings. This cannot be undone.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Third-party content

The game's music, "Space Fighter Loop" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), is licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is bundled with the app and no third-party service is contacted to play it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated first and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
